# Alexa Automation Architect artwork

**Concept — The routine drafting table.** People planning an Alexa routine move from a spoken cue to ordered actions and check the setup by hand. The folded paper expresses a blueprint the reader can take into the Alexa app. The waveform represents the proposed trigger phrase.

**Primary prompt.** Create a wide editorial still life on a midnight slate drafting table. Lay a folded cyan blueprint across its center with brass outlines of a lamp, an hourglass and a musical note, in sequence. Lead into the plan with a cyan waveform on tracing paper. Add a small pencil-marked checklist at right. Use grazing studio light, tactile paper and generous spacing. Keep all three actions legible at thumbnail size. Exclude lettering, logos, screens and people.

**Alternate prompt.** Draw a two-ink blueprint on dark navy graph paper. A folded sheet carries a waveform and three linked action medallions in warm brass. Tuck a cream checklist under the right edge. Use crisp drafting lines and strong silhouettes.

**Exact text in art.** None. The README supplies the title.

**Alt text.** A voice waveform leads into a folded routine blueprint showing a lamp, a wait and music beside a checklist.

**Delivery.** 1280 × 640 (2:1). Place banner.png above the README title. social-preview.png uses the same crop for manual upload. banner.svg is the editable, opaque-background fallback for dark and light themes.

**Refresh when** the blueprint sequence, phrase suggestions or manual checklist changes enough to make these motifs misleading.
